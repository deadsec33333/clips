How it works

One link in your bio. That is the whole setup.

Clips connects campaigns and clippers through Instagram. There are no accounts to link and no forms to fill in.

  1. Anyone can create a campaign

    Artists, creators, brands and communities. Launch a campaign and it goes live on Clips.

  2. Put your Clips link in your bio

    Creators and clippers both add their Clips link to their Instagram bio. That link is how they connect.

  3. Post clips

    Every clip counts toward the campaign, and so does the creator's own video.

  4. Clippers receive all of the fees

    The fee pool is shared between clippers by their share of total views. More views, bigger share.

Built-in distribution

Every clip also promotes the token.

Every clipper carries the Clips link in their bio, so every clip points viewers back to the campaign and its token. More clippers means more attention on the token.
