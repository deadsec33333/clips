Overview

Clips is a clipping platform where every campaign has its own token. Creators launch campaigns, clippers post clips of them, and clippers receive all of the fees, shared by their share of total views.

Clips serves three groups: clippers, campaign creators and traders. Other clipping platforms serve the first two.

How it works

  1. Anyone can create a campaign. It goes live on Clips with its own page and leaderboard.
  2. Creators and clippers put their Clips link in their Instagram bio. That link is how they connect. There is no account linking.
  3. Clippers post clips. Every clip counts toward the campaign, and so does the creator's own video.
  4. Clippers receive all of the fees, shared by their share of total views.

For clippers

Pick a campaign, put your Clips link in your Instagram bio and post clips. Your rank on the campaign leaderboard is set by your views.

How your payout is calculated

Your share of the fee pool equals your views divided by the total views of all clippers on that campaign. If a campaign has 1,000,000 total views and your clips have 100,000, you receive 10% of its fee pool.

For campaign creators

Launch a campaign with a name, an image and a tagline, then add your Instagram and your campaign video. Your own video counts as a clip. Every clipper who joins carries your link in their bio, so your reach grows with each one.

For traders

Every campaign has a token you can buy on Axiom or GMGN from its campaign page. Views and leaderboards are public, so you can see where attention is building.

Crypto assets are volatile and you can lose everything you put in. Nothing on Clips is financial advice.

The attention loop

More clippers bring more attention. More attention brings more buyers. More buyers can lift the token price, which means bigger payouts, which brings more clippers. Every clip also promotes the token, because every clipper has the link in their bio.

FAQ

Do I need to connect my Instagram account?

No. You put your Clips link in your bio. There is no account linking.

Who receives the fees?

Clippers receive all of the fees, shared by their share of total views on that campaign.

Does the creator's own video count?

Yes. The creator's own video counts as a clip too.

Where can I buy a campaign's token?

On Axiom or GMGN. Each campaign page links to both.
