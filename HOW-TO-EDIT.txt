CLIPS WEBSITE

Everything you normally change is at the top of index.html,
between "EDIT HERE" and "END OF EDIT AREA".
Open index.html with TextEdit (right click > Open With > TextEdit).

1. LINKS: replace [AXIOM LINK] and [GMGN LINK]. Add your X and Telegram
   links to make them appear in the side menu.
2. PARTNER: Tayo's photo is built in. Leave photo as "".
3. VIDEO: search for "PASTE INSTAGRAM EMBED HERE".
4. CAMPAIGNS: one block per campaign. feePool is in dollars, numbers only.
   ticker is optional, e.g. "KING".
5. LEADERBOARD: one line per clipper: { username: "name", views: 123000 },
   Set LEADERBOARD_IS_SAMPLE to false when the numbers are real.
6. LEGAL: fill in operator (company or person) and contact email.
   Have the Terms, Privacy and Risk pages checked before going public.
