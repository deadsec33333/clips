CLIPS WEBSITE

Everything you normally change is at the top of index.html,
between "EDIT HERE" and "END OF EDIT AREA".
Open index.html with TextEdit (right click > Open With > TextEdit).

BUY LINKS
  Replace [AXIOM LINK] and [GMGN LINK] with your real links. Keep the quotes.

CAMPAIGNS
  One line per campaign inside "const CAMPAIGNS = [ ... ]".
  Copy a line to add one, delete a line to remove one, keep the commas.
  sky can be: dawn, morning, noon, haze, dusk, night.
  Lines marked SAMPLE are placeholders.

VIDEO
  Search for "PASTE INSTAGRAM EMBED HERE".
  Delete the <div class="video-frame"> block under it and paste Instagram's embed code.
  On Vercel the embed plays normally.

UPDATING THE LIVE SITE
  After editing, drag this whole folder onto your Vercel project again
  (or run "vercel --prod" inside the folder). The link stays the same.
