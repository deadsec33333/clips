CLIPS WEBSITE

Everything you normally change is at the top of index.html,
between "EDIT HERE" and "END OF EDIT AREA".
Open index.html with TextEdit (right click > Open With > TextEdit).

TAYO'S PHOTO
  Put his picture in this folder and name it tayo.jpg.
  In index.html find PARTNER and change   photo:  ""   to   photo:  "tayo.jpg"

FEE POOL
  Each campaign has feePool: 25000 (dollars, numbers only, no $ or commas).
  Change the number. Dollars and percentages on the leaderboard update by themselves.

LEADERBOARD
  Find LEADERBOARD. Each line is one clipper:
  { username: "goalcam.edits", views: 1840000 },
  Change the name or views, copy a line to add a clipper, delete a line to remove one.
  When the numbers are real, change LEADERBOARD_IS_SAMPLE = true to false.

BUY LINKS
  Replace [AXIOM LINK] and [GMGN LINK] with your real links. Keep the quotes.

CAMPAIGNS
  One line per campaign inside "const CAMPAIGNS = [ ... ]".
  Lines marked SAMPLE are placeholders.

VIDEO
  Search for "PASTE INSTAGRAM EMBED HERE" and paste Instagram's embed code there.

UPDATING THE LIVE SITE
  Upload this whole folder to the same Vercel project again. The link stays the same.
